# Mission Reflection

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** because of its wide range of cloud services and its ability to support different types of workloads.

During this activity, I learned that AWS provides services for **computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and other cloud requirements**.

I also learned that **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** have many similarities. All three platforms provide **cloud computing, storage, networking, databases, identity management, and scalable infrastructure**.

Business requirements strongly influence the selection of a cloud platform because different organizations have different needs. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from **Microsoft Azure**, while an AI research company may prefer **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** because of its AI and machine learning capabilities.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. The recommended provider should match the client's **budget, existing technologies, workload requirements, scalability needs, security considerations, and business goals**.

My **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** also improved after completing this mission. I added **research, cloud platform comparisons, recommendations, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and technical documentation**.

I also gained more experience in organizing technical information using **Markdown and GitHub**.

Overall, this activity helped me understand how cloud platforms can be evaluated based on **real-world business requirements**. It also improved my ability to analyze cloud services and recommend an appropriate platform based on the **specific needs of a client**.
